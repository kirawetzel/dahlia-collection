About Me

As a certified Master Gardener through the University of California, my passion centers around educating my commnunity on sustainable home horticulture. More specifically, my interests are in sustainable regional cultivation, trial garden tracking, and maximizing soil health to produce fruit, vegetable, and flower crops with exceptional structural blooms. I'm also a partner to a wonderful husband, a dog mom, traveler, book reader, and during the day I work in technology leading a team of amazing engineers while also teaching Machine Learning to graduate students at UC Berkeley.

The "Golden Hour" Bouquets for Rescues

Dahlias are a hobby. Every single floral arrangement crafted from my cut garden goes directly toward making a difference. I sell seasonal handcrafted bouquets locally, and 100% of the profits are donated monthly to the senior fund of local Golden Retriever Rescue organizations to help fund medical care, supplies, and housing pipelines for senior dogs in need. At the end of the season, I dig & divide my tubers, doing a one time fall tuber sale, with proceeds that also go to a charity.
