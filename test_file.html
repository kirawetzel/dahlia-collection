Blooms with a Purpose

You're probably here because you've attended one of my Master Gardener workshops, scanned the QR code outside of my garden, or are on a tour of one of my gardens. Welcome! Feel free to explore the interactive directory below to see live locations, tracking statuses, and detailed notes on all the active cultivars currently blooming in the collection.

As a certified Master Gardener through the University of California, my passion centers around educating my community on sustainable home horticulture. More specifically, my interests are in sustainable regional cultivation, trial garden tracking, and maximizing soil health to produce fruit, vegetable, and flower crops. 2026 kicks off my journey in dahlia hybridizing.

I'm also a partner to a wonderful husband, a dog mom, traveler, book reader, and during the day I work in technology leading a team of amazing engineers while also teaching Machine Learning to graduate students at UC Berkeley.

Dahlias are a hobby for me. Every floral arrangement crafted from my cut garden goes directly toward making a difference. I sell seasonal handcrafted bouquets locally, and 100% of the profits are donated monthly to the senior fund of local Golden Retriever Rescue organizations.

Since this is a hobby, I don't sell tubers or cuttings throughout the year, however, at the end of the season, I hold a one-time fall tuber sale of things I won't grow anymore. Profits of this sale also go to charity.
